Sudar TNPSC App

What Data Will Be Deleted

If you wish to delete your Sudar TNPSC App account and permanently remove all your associated data, you can submit a deletion request through the contact method provided below.

When you submit an account deletion request, we will permanently delete the following data linked to your account:

  • Mobile number
  • User profile (if provided)
  • Test history
  • Test results and scores
  • Saved preferences
How Long Does It Take?
Once you submit the deletion request, your account and all associated data will be deleted within 7 working days.
How to Request Account Deletion
